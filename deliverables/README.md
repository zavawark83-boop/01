# Ring me — typography motion study

24-second portrait promotional concept, 1080×1920, 30fps, H.264 + AAC.
Original 120 BPM electronic soundtrack synthesized from oscillators and seeded noise; no sampled music or voiceover.

Open `index.html` in a browser or play `ringme-motion-v1.mp4` directly.
Render with `python3 render.py`. Requires Python, Pillow, NumPy, FFmpeg and the Noto Sans CJK / DejaVu Serif fonts at the paths in the script.
