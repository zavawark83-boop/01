# Ring me — product motion v2

24 seconds, 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264 video and stereo AAC audio.

The supplied reference recording was inspected. This revision uses the four actual supplied app screenshots, perspective phone motion, projected floating interface cards, light/dark juxtaposition, red/pink/white cuts, and large Korean typography. The soundtrack is an original 120 BPM electronic composition with transition sweeps and impact accents; no audio from the reference was copied.

Phone screen content is cropped only to remove Android system bars and fitted into animated device frames. Profile text, portraits and app content come from the supplied captures. Floating cards are crops of the same supplied captures. The English Ring me end wordmark is cropped from the real app header; the decorative red ring is an original vector motif, not the original red-cord logo file. These are animated screenshots, not a recording of live app interactions.

`render_v2.py` is the reproducible compositor in the working project, with `render.py` providing shared typography helpers and the original score. The EGG archive contained four stored JPEG blocks; sizes and CRC32 checksums were verified during extraction. User reference footage is not redistributed.
